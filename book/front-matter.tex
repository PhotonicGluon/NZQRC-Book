% Author's Note
\chapter{Author's Note}
The genesis for this book began back in late September 2024. The idea was to write a book that explains looks at how we can define all the numbers that are used today from the simple axioms of Zermelo-Fraenkel Set Theory.

However, for multiple reasons, I did not go ahead and finish the book. I finished the first two parts on the foundations of axiomatic arithmetic and the natural numbers, but I struggled to continue the momentum with the other parts of the ladder. I could not properly motivate the integers, the rationals, the real numbers, and the complex numbers. As such, this project was put on hiatus since December 2024.

Now, as the 2-year anniversary of beginning this book approaches, I felt that it would be a waste to let whatever I've written be hidden away much longer. Therefore, I am finally releasing this book to the world, as incomplete as it is. I hope whatever I've written is useful to someone. As the old proverb goes: "\textit{One man's trash is another man's treasure}". I hope you'll enjoy, despite the lack of completeness.

\hfill{\textit{7 September, 2026}}
