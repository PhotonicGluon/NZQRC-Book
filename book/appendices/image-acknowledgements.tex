\chapter{Image Acknowledgements}

\section*{Non-Original Images}
For these images, they follow the following format.
\begin{itemize}
    \item Each attribution begins with a short description of what the image is.
    \item \code{Author} is the person who created the image, took the photo, or uploaded the photo.
    \item \code{Licence/Status} is the copyright status of the image, or a licence if applicable.
    \item \code{Source} is the source of the image.
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \code{Wikimedia} refers to the Wikimedia Commons. The general format for images from Wikimedia commons is \\\code{https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:NAME}.
        \item \code{Flickr} refers to \url{https://flickr.com/}. The general format for images from Flickr is \\\code{https://flickr.com/photos/USER\_ID/FILE\_ID}.
        \item \code{Flaticon} refers to \url{https://www.flaticon.com/}. The general format for images from Flaticon is \\\code{https://www.flaticon.com/BLURB}.
    \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}
